Keeping only 15 to 20 of 200 bands

Result. With a supervised search, 15 to 20 bands (7.5 % to 10 % of the spectrum) score within about 1 point of all 200 bands, averaged over 6 different train/test splits. Compared with the cleaned 164-band set the loss is about 2 to 3 points. Label-free methods lose 1.7 to 4.6 points against all 200 bands.

4. Limits

  • The splits are random pixel splits, so neighbouring pixels from one field can be on both sides; this lifts all methods equally.
  • The polish step was tuned on training pixels only but adds just 0.3 to 1 point, which is small compared with split-to-split spread.
  • Band sets that were picked for the recommendation (the polished 15, 18 and 20 band sets of split 42) are in recommended_band_sets_original_numbers.json.
